## Fractional CTO for Seed and Series A startups (Utah and remote)

I work with Seed and Series A founders in Utah (Silicon Slopes and Salt Lake City) and remotely to provide senior technical leadership, stabilize AI-built or contractor MVPs, and architect production systems without the $250,000+ commitment of a full-time VP of Engineering.

If you run a non-tech operational business in Utah and want to automate manual workflows, visit my commercial studio [UtahDevCo](https://www.utahdevco.com) ($0-down Workflow & ROI Audit, 50% Year-1 savings pricing).

### Frequently asked questions

#### When should a startup hire a Fractional CTO instead of a full-time CTO?
Hire a Fractional CTO when you need senior architectural decisions, hiring help, or codebase stabilization today, but do not yet have the funding or team size (typically under 8 to 10 engineers) to justify a $250k+ executive salary and significant equity grant.

#### Can you stabilize an AI-built or contractor-built Next.js app?
Yes. A large share of my advisory work involves auditing prototypes built with AI coding tools or offshore contractors. I fix authentication and authorization gaps, database schema issues, and deployment reliability without throwing away working product value.
