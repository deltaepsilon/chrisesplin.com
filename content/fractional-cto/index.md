### Frequently Asked Questions

#### When should a startup hire a Fractional CTO instead of a full-time CTO?
Hire a Fractional CTO when you need senior architectural decisions, hiring help, or codebase stabilization today, but don't yet have the funding or team size (typically under 8–10 engineers) to justify a $250k+ executive salary and significant equity grant.

#### Can you stabilize an AI-built or contractor-built Next.js app?
Yes. A large share of my advisory work involves auditing prototypes built with AI coding tools or offshore contractors—fixing authentication/authorization gaps, database schema issues, and deployment reliability without throwing away working product value.
